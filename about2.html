About

Compassion is the core of my work

Offering clients a soft place to land when things are hard, that's my ethos, in every session, every modality, every conversation.

Personal Statement

A genuine passion for healing

I have a genuine passion for supporting healing, personal growth, self-discovery and recovery from within. At the heart of my work is the professional, warm relationship I build with my clients, a relationship of mutual trust and safety based on a strong sense of compassion.

I aim to provide a compassion focused, trauma informed therapeutic approach that seeks to understand and respond to the impact of trauma on people's lives. This emphasises physical, psychological and emotional safety, supporting you to re-establish control, connection and safety in your own life.

I hold a firm belief that, given the right conditions and support, each person holds the inherent ability to heal. My role is to understand you, appreciate your personality, listen without judgement, and use my skills in holistic and psychological therapies to help you find a way forward together.

Values

Soft but assertive, always

My tone of voice, and the way I hold a room, is soft but assertive. Talking can feel too clinical for some, so my work blends gentler modalities with clinical rigour, creating a space where you feel supported, safe and held in your pain, whatever that pain looks like.

Written Work

A published writer and author

I am a published writer, contributing regular mental health articles and blogs for local and national publications including Mindsum.org, Belfast Live Psychology Net, and RSVP Magazine. In 2024 I released my first self development book, A Stripper Called Bambi, which reached No.3 in Amazon's Health & Lifestyle bestsellers.

My studies into how trauma impacts the body also led me to found Newtownabbey's first infrared sauna studio, Elements Wellness Studio, offering heat, light and salt therapy alongside talking and somatic work.

My Practice

Two therapists, one gentle practice

Alongside me, my associate counsellor Andrea Black holds her own sessions at the clinic, so you can choose the therapist who feels right for you.

Associate Counsellor

Andrea Black MBACP

Therapy offers a calm space to make sense of what feels heavy. My clients come to me with anxiety, low mood, stress, trauma, relationship struggles, or simply a sense that something isn't right. I help them slow down, understand their emotions and find steadier ground.

I use an integrative approach, drawing on person centred, psychodynamic and CBT practice, and I tailor therapy to each person's needs, pace and goals. Together we explore what's going on, build emotional understanding and develop practical tools for coping and resilience, so you leave feeling steadier, clearer and more able to move forward.

With more than 15 years of experience and over 6,000 supervised clinical hours, I've worked with people through historical issues, grief and loss, relationship and family difficulties, and the pressures of everyday life such as work stress, caring responsibilities and burnout. I believe we all deserve a safe place to talk, and more importantly, to feel heard.

"Counselling is always a brave choice, giving you the time to explore your options in a safe, non judgemental, caring space." (Andrea Black)

I manage my own practice and diary, and I'm available in the clinic on Tuesday mornings, Wednesday evenings and weekends.
