My Approach

Healing works with the whole of you

Rather than focusing on mental health alone, my bespoke approach works with the four bodies of a person: emotional, mental, physical and spiritual. It's shaped to your personality and beliefs, because sometimes talking is simply too much, and you deserve a gentler way in.

The Lotus

Why a lotus flower

In Buddhism, the lotus is a symbol of awakening. It grows in muddy water and rises above the surface to bloom in beauty and purity, which is exactly how I see the therapeutic journey. The closed bud represents potential. The open flower represents enlightenment.

Despite challenging beginnings, one can rise above and emerge whole.

The different stages of the lotus mirror the different stages of therapy, and it's a reminder I keep close in every session I hold.
