Therapy that adapts to you

As an integrated therapist, I bring counselling, somatic experiencing, wellness coaching and reiki together into one bespoke framework, so the therapy aligns with you rather than expecting you to adapt to it.

01 · Talking Therapy

Counselling

Counselling is a confidential talking therapy. Talking through problems allows time and space to process situations and relationships, and to make sense of feelings that can otherwise feel overwhelming.

I incorporate Person Centred Counselling, CBT, Solution Focused Therapy, Psychodynamic Therapy and Somatic Experiencing into sessions, blending these theories to foster self acceptance and strengthen your sense of self. I work with clients of all ages and backgrounds, from short single sessions to long term, ongoing support.

02 · Body Based Therapy

Somatic Experiencing

Somatic Experiencing is a body oriented therapy developed by Dr Peter Levine to help process and heal from trauma. It works on the premise that trauma is stored in the body, and can show up as physical tension, emotional distress or a dysregulated nervous system.

By focusing on bodily sensations, you learn to become more aware of your physical experience, releasing pent up energy and emotion associated with past trauma, while developing tools to regulate your nervous system and find greater balance.

03 · Coaching & Wellbeing

Wellness Coaching

Should you prefer a more solution focused, directive approach, coaching may be for you. It's ideal if you're working towards a specific goal or life change, such as a career move, but need help figuring out exactly what that change is and how to get there.

Together we discuss the advantages and disadvantages of your options and build an action plan to reach your goal in the most efficient, rewarding way possible.

04 · Energy Healing

Reiki

Reiki is an energy based therapy that activates the body's natural ability to heal itself, restoring both physical and emotional wellbeing. A treatment eliminates stress and promotes deep relaxation, bringing a sense of balance, clarity and harmony from within.

Trained to Master Level, having taught over 300 students, I often incorporate reiki within counselling sessions, or offer it as a standalone, non-talking treatment for anyone who could use a little peace.
