Good to Know

Terms of business

How does the first session work?

At an initial consultation, we'll explore your current issues together and establish the focus and plan for your therapy. It helps if you can let me know the nature of what you're dealing with, and your preferred appointment time, when you first get in touch.

What's your cancellation policy?

Missed sessions, or sessions cancelled within 24 hours, are chargeable in full. This helps protect time set aside for both of us.

Can I speak to someone before committing?

Yes. Before committing to therapy, you're welcome to request a short chat to discuss your options, at a time that suits you.

What if my availability doesn't work for the diary?

My team of recommended associates, including Andrea Black, are available so you're never left without support.
