Why I Wrote It

Rebranding yourself, one honest story at a time

As a psychotherapeutic counsellor, I spend my days helping clients set down the roles and expectations that no longer fit them. This book grew out of that same work, told through my own life, my own missteps and my own becoming.

Alongside this book, I write regular mental health articles and blogs for local and national publications, including Mindsum.org, Belfast Live Psychology Net and RSVP Magazine. Writing, much like therapy, is simply another way of helping people feel a little less alone.
